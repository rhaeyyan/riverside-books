**Project 1: Direct-to-Consumer Retail**

## **Business Description**

Riverside Books is a single-location independent bookstore that sells new books, cards, and small gifts, and hosts occasional author events. The owner runs day-to-day operations with two part-time booksellers, and currently manages inventory, orders, and customer communication through a mix of memory, sticky notes, and a basic spreadsheet. The store wants to modernize the customer experience and staff operations without turning into a large e-commerce business: customers still shop primarily by walking in or calling ahead.

## **Business Model Type**

Direct sale (retail). The store sells physical products, books and gifts, and event tickets directly to individual customers and is paid at the point of sale, either online for a pre-order or in person at checkout.

## **Customers & Users**

Two groups: Customers are local residents and regulars who browse in-store or call ahead to check stock, place a pre-order, or ask about upcoming events. Staff are the owner and two part-time booksellers who manage inventory, fulfill orders, and answer customer questions throughout the day.

## **Common Pain Points**

* Customers have no way to check if a specific book is in stock before making a trip to the store, or to place a pre-order online.  
* There is no loyalty or rewards system, so regulars have no reason to keep track of their purchases with this store specifically.  
* Staff track inventory levels by memory or a paper log, so no one notices a book is out of stock until a customer asks for it.  
* Common customer questions, such as store hours, return policy, and the event schedule, get asked repeatedly and pull staff away from the register.  
* The store posts to social media inconsistently because writing captions takes time nobody has.

## **Product Suite**

*Each teammate builds one of these four products.*

**Product A: Customer Ordering & Loyalty App**

Lets a customer search the catalog, see what is currently in stock, place a pre-order for pickup, and earn a stamp toward a loyalty reward with each purchase.

**Product B: Staff Inventory & Ops Dashboard**

Gives staff a live view of stock levels by title, flags titles that are low or out of stock, and lists pending pre-orders that need to be prepared.

**Product C: Customer Support Chatbot**

Answers customer questions using the store's actual current inventory, hours, and policies, so it can say whether a specific title is in stock right now instead of just answering generic FAQs.

**Product D: Marketing Content Generator**

Given a specific book or upcoming event, generates a short social media caption and post idea that staff can review and publish.
